Inherited limitations

an inherited chunk hash mismatch. Their original hashes are recorded and
their bytes have deliberately not been rewritten. The original fitted outfit
patches carry this limitation. Preserving them is not a claim that the
upstream checksums are valid.

match their patch-manifest hashes. The three other upstream Anca outfit
originals were unavailable for combination checks. Their installer conditions
are covered, but their full combinations remain unvalidated.
