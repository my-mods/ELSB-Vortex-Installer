Other body replacements
Choose that replacement or the corresponding ELSB body unless a specific
compatibility patch supports both. Unrelated mods can remain enabled.
New mods remain separate Vortex entries; the catalogue is extended only
after inspecting the specific combination.

Conflict interpretation
Different physical container names can contain the same Unreal asset path.
Vortex's ordinary file-conflict display does not reveal every such overlap.
Our patches use ELSB's earlier-name priority convention. Do not rename their
containers. If another mod uses an earlier name for the same asset, its
interaction needs review. A physical overwrite rule alone cannot merge
Unreal meshes or tables. The audit reports overlaps, not guaranteed fit.

Original ELSB checksums
Original payload hashes are preserved. Of 276 original ELSB container
variants, 261 have inherited retoc checksum failures. These have not been
silently rewritten. The 24 personal add-on/new patch variants verify.
This distinction is provenance and package evidence, not gameplay approval.
