ELSB character compatibility catalogue

The Characters category review covers the 31 original entries listed below.
Install only the originals for the choices you want. Each remains a separate
Vortex entry; selecting a patch does not install or activate its original.
The wizard names the supported archive and retains the previous choice names.

Lacra Beautiful Hair v4
Select Lacra Beautiful Hair - clothing-table patch under Lacra hair. It keeps
the selected ELSB/Pussy Walker body, outfit and scene settings and adds the
physics-hair slot. All 928 ELSB clothing rows remain intact; its hair row is
added separately. Eight appearance presets are merged for All scenes and
Nude outfit. The downloaded v4 payload, current Vortex original and retained
fixed-v2 payload match byte for byte. Keep just one hair original enabled.

Body and outfit alternatives
Thicc Lacra and SlimThicc Lacra are alternative Lacra body selections.
They retain the chosen original Lacra shape while other characters keep ELSB.
They do not combine two different Lacra body shapes. Their two clothing rows
are merged into the selected ELSB table, with a separate physics-hair variant.
Their different torso hide masks are preserved. Use one of these originals
at a time; ELSB Lacra outfits and the ELSB Croft head route are unavailable
with those alternative bodies.
Coen Nude, Revealing Anca, Revealing Lacra, Loose Anca, Revealing Thicc Anca
and Thicc Anca retain their existing ELSB fitted patches. Their required
originals match the supplied fit manifests. Anca outfits remain alternatives.
For Pussywalker, select the included ELSB + Pussy Walker body separately for
Anca and/or Lacra and keep standalone VaginaMod disabled. That standalone
shared-body replacement is not a dependency of the adapted personal bodies.

Face, skin and hair choices
Choose one original per skin/face category. Coen face and Lacra face, surface
and wrinkle selections are separate, so they can accompany a body texture.
Their narrow priority patches retain the original texture content. Anca Puffy
Hair preserves the original cloth asset and replaces the ELSB hairstyle choice.
Rise of Lacra Croft routes the ELSB presets to the original replacement head;
the torso, limbs and selected story/scene materials remain unchanged.
The personal Pussy Walker graft uses its own skin atlas. A full recolour such
as Gothic Lacra does not recolour that private graft atlas; use the regular
ELSB Lacra body for a consistent Gothic skin setup.

Conflict interpretation
Matching Unreal asset paths can conflict even when physical container names
differ. Do not rename patch containers. Patches use the established ELSB name
priority convention; the optional read-only audit lists deployed originals
at known hashes and internal overlaps. It also recognizes different cooked
project folder names that mount to the same /Game path.
Original body/face alternatives are not automatically disabled by the wizard.
New originals or different asset content require another review.

Evidence and limits
Native container checks, independent cooked-property comparisons and installer
plans are documented in Verification.txt. Original ELSB files remain unchanged,
including their inherited checksum failures. Animation, skin seams, head/neck
alignment, hair physics, outfit clipping and scene transitions need gameplay
acceptance. This catalogue does not claim every combination has been played.
